      How the planner decides

      1. Scans the strongest stocks (Nifty 50, Sensex, your watchlist, Nifty/Sensex options) with the same rules as the stock page. · 2. Sizes each trade so the loss at stop-loss stays within your "max loss per trade" and capital within "max capital per trade". · 3. Expected P&L = win-chance × net profit at target 1 − loss-chance × net loss at stop-loss, after all charges. Win-chance comes from your Scorecard once it has 20+ results; until then a cautious default is used. · 4. Only positive expected-P&L trades are shown. ★ marks a set that fits your balance together.
      Rules, not guarantees. Expected P&L is an average over many trades; any single trade can hit its stop-loss.
